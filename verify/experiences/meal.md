# Experience: a meal travels through the body

Who is watching: someone with no medical training. What they should come away with, in one sentence:
"The food went in, sugar went from the gut to the liver and blood, the pancreas sent insulin, the muscles and liver soaked the sugar up, and then everything calmed down."

How it should feel throughout: like looking inside a real body. Anatomical shapes and real tissue colours, things flowing through real vessels, never flat cartoon blobs, floating dots or labels doing the work. Calm at rest, clearly busier after eating, calm again later.

## Beats

Each beat names when, where on the body, what you see, and what would make it fail.

| # | When | Where | What you should see | Fails if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before eating | Whole belly | A quiet body: gut still, blood flowing at an even pace, nothing highlighted | Something already glows or pulses, so there is no "before" |
| 2 | 0 to 5 min | Stomach | The stomach visibly holds the meal (fuller, tinted as food) and starts to churn | The meal is only a number in a panel |
| 3 | 5 to 20 min | Small intestine, then the portal vein to the liver | A stream of food-sugar leaves the gut wall into the vein that runs to the liver; the liver lights up before the rest of the blood does | Sugar appears everywhere at once, or the stream does not follow a real vessel |
| 4 | 5 to 20 min | Pancreas | The pancreas visibly answers (pulse or glow) shortly after sugar starts rising | The pancreas looks the same as at rest, or something else (the gut) is what lights up |
| 5 | 15 to 60 min | Blood, then muscles (thigh, arm) and liver | Insulin travels in the blood (shown exaggerated and labelled "not to scale") and the muscles and liver glow as they take sugar in | Muscles do not change, or the glow is a flat tint with no sense of uptake |
| 6 | 30 to 60 min | Top bar and chart | Blood sugar peaks, and the peak lines up in time with the busiest moment on the body | The number moves but the body does not, or they peak at different times |
| 7 | 2 to 3 h | Whole belly | Things settle back: stomach emptier, glows fade, pace back to calm | It stays lit up, or it snaps back instantly |

## Evidence the judge gets

- A screenshot at each beat time, from the belly view and the whole-body view.
- A 6-second clip at beats 3, 4 and 5 (motion matters there).
- This page. The judge must not see the code or the engine's numbers.

## How it is judged

For each beat: present / missing / wrong place / wrong order, with the frame that shows it. Then three overall scores out of 5: understandable to a non-medical person, looks like a real body, matches the one-sentence takeaway. The scenario passes only if every beat is present and in order and no overall score is below 4. A person signs off last.
